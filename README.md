
# Awesome Student Modeling [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated list of **awesome resources, papers, and tools** for researchers and practitioners interested in **student modeling**.

Student modeling is the process of representing a learner's state—including their knowledge, skills, preferences, and behaviors—to facilitate personalized and adaptive learning experiences. This repository provides a comprehensive collection of foundational works and cutting-edge research in the field.

## Datasets & Benchmarks
- (*EDM 2020*) EdNet: A Large-Scale Hierarchical Dataset in Education [[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.03072)][[Leaderboard](http://ednet-leaderboard.s3-website-ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com/)]
- (*JEDM 2021*) The ASSISTments Dataset: A Longitudinal Dataset of Student Learning [[paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/3375462.3375523)][[dataset](https://sites.google.com/site/assistmentsdata/)]
- (*EDM 2024*) **SingPAD**: A Knowledge Tracing Dataset Based on Music Performance Assessment [[paper](https://dblp.org/rec/conf/edm/ZhangZXZWS24)].
- (*ACL 2024*) **StudentEval**: A Benchmark of Student-Written Prompts for Large Language Models of Code [[paper](https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.120/)].

## Foundational Papers
- (*Cognitive Science 1995*) Knowledge Tracing: Modeling the Acquisition of Procedural Knowledge [[paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01099821)]
  - *Summary*: Introduces the knowledge-tracing model, a foundational approach in student modeling.

## 2025 Highlights (New!)

### Research Papers
- (*EDM 2025*) **A Multi-View Predictive Student Modeling Framework with Interpretable Causal Graph Discovery for Collaborative Learning Analytics** - Acosta et al.
- (*AIED 2025*) **Understanding Student Engagement with Large Language Model-Powered Course Assistants** - Liu et al.
- (*ACL 2025*) **QG-SMS: Enhancing Test Item Analysis via Student Modeling and Simulation** - Nguyen et al.
- (*NeurIPS 2025*) **Personalized Exercise Recommendation with Semantically-Grounded Knowledge Tracing** - introduces *ExRec* framework for structured learning progression.
- (*IJCAI 2025*) **Contrastive Cross-Course Knowledge Tracing via Concept Graph Guided Knowledge Transfer** - introduces *TransKT* for cross-course behavior modeling.
- (*Survey 2025*) **A Systematic Review of Deep Knowledge Tracing (2015-2025): Toward Responsible AI for Education**.

### Tools & Trends
- **Agentic Learning Companions**: LLMs as teachable agents and personalized tutors (e.g., *HypoCompass*).
- **Responsible AI**: Focus on fairness metrics for Bayesian Knowledge Tracing and gender bias in collaboration analytics.

## Tools & Libraries
- **[OpenLearnerModel](https://github.com/openlearnermodel)** - A toolkit for building learner models for adaptive educational systems.
- **[PyBKT](https://github.com/CAHLR/pyBKT)** - A Python implementation of Bayesian Knowledge Tracing for analyzing student learning data.

## Knowledge Tracing
Modeling the progression of student knowledge over time.

- (*Cognitive Science 1995*) **Knowledge Tracing: Modeling the Acquisition of Procedural Knowledge** [[paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01099821)]
- (*NeurIPS 2015*) **Deep Knowledge Tracing** [[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.05908)]
- (*EDM 2024*) **Practical Evaluation of Deep Knowledge Tracing Models for use in Learning Platforms** [[paper](https://educationaldatamining.org/edm2024/proceedings/)]

## Learner & User Modeling
Broader representations including preferences, affect, and lifelong learning.

- (*Survey 2024*) **User Modeling and Profiling: A Comprehensive Survey** [[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10101)]
- (*UMAP 2025*) **Sentence Encoder-Based Clustering Method for Modeling Students' Learning Programming Behavior** - Kamberovic et al.
- (*Lifelong 2026/Forthcoming*) **User Lifelong Behavior Modeling in Large-Scale Recommender Systems** - addressing ultra-long heterogeneous sequences.

## Intelligent Textbooks
Adaptive digital textbooks that utilize student modeling for personalization.

- (*AAAI 2024*) **AI-driven Personalization in Digital Textbooks** - adaptive recommendations based on real-time behavior.
- (*AIED 2024*) **HypoCompass: Using LLMs as a Teachable Agent for Debugging** [[paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00000)]
- (*AQG 2025*) **Enhancing Test Item Analysis via Student Modeling and Simulation** - Nguyen et al. (ACL 2025).
